# QA round - novice warehouse staff, 360 px phone (main 7fdbbc8)

Users: staff, clerk (demo). Test data: remarks start with "QA-novice". DEMO-002 net stock change = 0 (received 510, issued 510). Clerk draft RCV-0225 cancelled by me. Posted docs RCV-0210, RCV-0216, ISS-0036/37/38 remain (cannot be deleted, only reversed by a supervisor).
Note: tabs_create / tabs_context were not available; I used the only existing tab ("seed") and reset the viewport at the end.

Ranked findings
1. Major - every page: scroll position is kept when you move to another page. Opening Receipts, Issues, Home, "My drafts" lands mid-list or with the title/back link/"New receipt" button cut off by the header. Repro: scroll down in a list, tap another bottom tab. Expected: new page starts at top.
2. Major - Products list (and any paged list): tapping Next page leaves you at the bottom of page 2 (same scroll offset); 25 cards x ~290 px = ~7000 px of scrolling per page. Rows-per-page and First/Last are hidden at 360 px (exist in the DOM), so you cannot choose a smaller page. No column menu on phone.
3. Major - "Submit & post" label: after tapping, the doc becomes "Submitted" with the text "Ready to post" and a second "Post?" dialog opens 1-2 s later with no spinner. If you close that dialog nothing is posted and the status word "Submitted" looks finished. Novice expects one tap = stock moved. Suggest spinner/disable on the button, or label "Next: review and post".
4. Major - "Edit" on a Submitted document silently turns it back into Draft (no confirm, no toast), the page keeps the Edit/Post buttons layout changed; "Submit & post" in the footer then wraps to two lines next to the "..." button.
5. Minor/Major - Home tile "My drafts 47" opens the Receipts "Mine" list with no draft filter (shows Posted docs, receipts only). Count does not match what you see; issue drafts are not shown.
6. Minor - Receipts/Issues lists: 47 identical-looking draft cards (number, date, warehouse, "Total qty 1"), no product name or remark; cannot tell which draft is which without opening each.
7. Minor - Empty-lines error on Submit ("Add at least one item.") renders below the fold under the empty-state illustration; at first tap nothing visibly happens. Qty error ("Enter a quantity above 0.") wraps to 3 lines and shifts the -/+ buttons; footer still says "0 lines | 0 units" while a line exists.
8. Minor - Date filter: typing 17102026 (no slashes) is rejected with "Enter a date as dd/mm/yyyy, such as 09/10/2026." (no auto-slash mask). Messages for invalid (31/02/2026) and reversed range ("From must be on or before To.", shown under To, plus same text in the empty state) are clear. Invalid date is silently ignored by the list (list stays unfiltered while error shows). Malay placeholder reads hh/bb/tttt, not dd/mm/yyyy (requirement says dd/MM/yyyy everywhere).
9. Minor - Jargon: purpose "Use" (use for what?), badges "Large" / "Override" / "Safety 100" / unlabeled red number on Low stock cards (6, 100, 20...), "Reversed" (Malay "Dibatalkan kesan"), "Posted". "Ready to post" is shown to a clerk who cannot post.
10. Minor - Fractional qty 12.5 PCS accepted for a bolt (PCS) with no warning; Supplier is a plain dropdown with ~100 entries incl. very long names, not searchable. Add-item search "bo" ranks SKU-substring noise (E2E-...BO...) after the real match.

Nit - Low-stock tile arrow icon is the same as the Receipts icon; "..." more button is a faint 3 dots; stock cards differ in layout (DEMO-001 has no warehouse line); More sheet scrolls the page behind; account button is a bare person icon (sign-out is two taps deep, no confirmation, no "signed out" message - acceptable).
