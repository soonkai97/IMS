#### Contract changes for the frontend (money precision)
- **`DocumentLineDto` (every document read and write).** `unitCost` is now the cost per ENTERED unit (the unit in `uomCode`), money, 2 decimals: what was typed, so `qty x unitCost` is the line `amount`. New `baseUnitCost` (the cost per BASE unit, a unit price, up to 4 decimals: 1 carton of 3 at 10.00 is 3.3333; a carton of 1,000 at 3.00 is 0.0030) and `amount` (the stored line amount, money). All three are `null` without `cost.view`. **The editor must load `unitCost` into the cost field and send it back as `unitCost`; never `baseUnitCost`.** (The editor used to load the per-base value, which re-priced a line in a non-base unit on every save: 1 carton of 3 at 10.00 came back as 3.33.) For an issue and for a receipt saved by the Warehouse staff (which take the average) `unitCost` is the average per entered unit and is not sent back.
- **Totals.** `totalAmount` (document and list) is the sum of the line `amount`s, each rounded to a cent. The web footer total (a floating-point sum of `qty x unitCost` over the lines) can differ from the API by floating-point noise or, for a sub-cent product, by a cent: show the API `totalAmount` after a save, and compute the footer as the sum of `round(qty x unitCost, 2)` per line, half away from zero, in decimal arithmetic.
- **Money fields, 2 decimals:** `amount`, `totalAmount`, stock `value`, `totalValue`, approval `amountThreshold` and the trigger numbers of an amount rule, `unitCost` of a document line. **Unit prices, up to 4 decimals:** `baseUnitCost`, stock row `avgCost`, ledger `unitCost`, movements `unitCost`. Show a unit price with up to 4 decimals (trailing zeros may be trimmed to 2), an amount with exactly 2.
- **Input.** A receipt line `unitCost` with more than 2 decimals is a `400` "Unit cost can have at most 2 decimal places." with one field error per line (`Data.Lines[n].UnitCost`) for a caller who may enter cost (Clerk, Supervisor, Owner, Admin). It is NOT judged on an issue or on a request of the Warehouse staff, where the cost is ignored. A negative cost is a `400` with one message ("Unit cost cannot be negative."). A line whose amount would exceed 999,999,999,999,999 is a `400` ("The amount of a line cannot be more than 999,999,999,999,999."). Approval `amountThreshold` with more than 2 decimals: `400` "An amount can have at most 2 decimal places.". Limit the inputs to 2 decimals (`step="0.01"`).
- **Imports.** The opening-stock and product imports read the number in a cell, not the text it shows (a cell formatted `0.0` that holds 2.505 is refused; 1,234.50 is accepted; a formula is read by its result). A sub-cent cost is a row error.
- **Excel.** Money cells ("Value", "Amount", the template's "UnitCost") are numbers formatted `#,##0.00`; unit-price cells ("Avg cost" and "Unit cost" of the stock summary, ledger and movements exports) are numbers formatted `#,##0.0000`. The cells hold the rounded numbers.
- **Interaction with the average-cost change** (`feature/avg-cost-and-money-precision`): that change keeps the average and the cost per base unit with 10 decimals inside the database; this one decides what leaves the API.
