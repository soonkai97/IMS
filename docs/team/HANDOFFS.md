- Numbers are `{type: integer|number, format}`; the `["integer","string"]` arrays and the digit `pattern` are gone.

**Timestamps**: every instant (`createdAt`, `submittedAt`, `approvedAt`, `postedAt`, `returnedAt`, ledger `occurredAt`, audit `at`, movements `postedAt`, login `expiresAt`, ...) is `yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm:ss[.fffffff]Z`.
