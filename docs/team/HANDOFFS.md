#### Contract changes for the frontend (money precision)
- **`DocumentLineDto` (every document read and write).** `unitCost` is now the cost per ENTERED unit (the unit in `uomCode`), money, 2 decimals: what was typed, so `qty x unitCost` is the line `amount`. New `baseUnitCost` (the cost per BASE unit, a unit price, up to 4 decimals: 1 carton of 3 at 10.00 is 3.3333; a carton of 1,000 at 3.00 is 0.0030) and `amount` (the stored line amount, money). All three are `null` without `cost.view`. **The editor must load `unitCost` into the cost field and send it back as `unitCost`; never `baseUnitCost`.** (The editor used to load the per-base value, which re-priced a line in a non-base unit on every save: 1 carton of 3 at 10.00 came back as 3.33.) **A line valued at the average (every issue line; a receipt saved by someone who may not enter cost) has `costFromAverage: true` and `unitCost: null`**: there is no cost to edit. Show its `baseUnitCost` (the average) and `amount` read-only and send no cost for it: a `PUT` where the cost is omitted keeps the line at the average, so a Warehouse staff receipt is never re-priced by someone who opens and saves it (an average of 0.0030 stays 0.0030, 1.2349 x 1,000 stays 1,234.90). A cost typed on such a line makes it a typed cost (`costFromAverage` false). Known and unchanged from main: a Warehouse staff re-save of a draft replaces a cost a Supervisor typed on it with the average (staff may not enter cost). The amount of a flagged draft is re-valued at the current average on every save and at post, so a draft read earlier can be stale.
- **Totals.** `totalAmount` (document and list) is the sum of the line `amount`s, each rounded to a cent. The web footer total (a floating-point sum of `qty x unitCost` over the lines) can differ from the API by floating-point noise or, for a sub-cent product, by a cent, and it is wrong for a line at the average (`unitCost` is null there): the footer is the sum of the API line `amount`s (and `totalAmount` after a save); for a line being edited and not saved yet, `round(qty x unitCost, 2)` in decimal arithmetic, half away from zero. A line amount above 999,999,999,999,999 is a `400` ("The amount of a line cannot be more than 999,999,999,999,999.").
- **Money fields, 2 decimals:** `amount`, `totalAmount`, stock `value`, `totalValue`, approval `amountThreshold` and the trigger numbers of an amount rule, `unitCost` of a document line. **Unit prices, up to 4 decimals:** `baseUnitCost`, stock row `avgCost`, ledger `unitCost`, movements `unitCost`.
